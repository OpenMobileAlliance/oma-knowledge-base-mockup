# 🚀 PubHub — From GitHub to Published Specs

**Publication Hub (PubHub)** is a GitHub-native publishing engine that transforms raw Markdown into professional-grade outputs — including **PDF**, **HTML**, and **Open Document Format (ODF)**. Designed for **standards organizations, working groups, and spec authors**, **PubHub** helps you move from draft to publication without leaving your repository.

Whether you're collaborating across teams or preparing documents for ISO submission, **PubHub** automates formatting and structure so you can focus on the content — not the tooling.

We’re actively developing new features that make publishing structured documents faster, more intuitive, and deeply collaborative.

---

### 🌟 Why **PubHub**?

✅ Publish to PDF, HTML, and ODF — straight from Markdown  
✅ Built for editors and contributors — not just developers  
✅ Automatically builds from every commit  
✅ Designed for standardization workflows and spec governance  
✅ No more copy/paste formatting or external publishing delays
